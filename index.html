HOW TO PLAY

1 · Play the four lanes

W up · A left · S down · D right. Press a lane's key when its note reaches the ring.
Gold link = press together · long bar = hold to the end.

2 · A miss brings her closer

One miss and she takes a step. Missing in a row makes her faster.
Four misses in a row and she charges!

3 · Keep the rhythm, keep the spell

A long combo keeps her spellbound and pushes her back.
Survive to the final note to complete the performance.

TIMING CALIBRATION

Press Start, then press SPACE on every tick (16 ticks).
The game measures how early or late you press on average.
