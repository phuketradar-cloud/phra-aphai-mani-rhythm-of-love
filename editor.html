Mouse & keys

  • Click an empty spot in a row: add a note (row = difficulty; inside a row the lanes W/A/S/D are stacked top to bottom).
  • Click a note: select · Drag: move · Del/right-click: remove
  • WASD move the selected note to that lane (nothing selected: lane for new notes)
  • 123 difficulty · H hold on/off · [] hold length · X strong beat
  • Chord = notes at the same time in different lanes (gold outline = chord group)
  • ←→ nudge selected 5 ms (Shift 1 ms)
  • Space play/pause · click the ruler: seek
  • Mouse wheel: scroll · Ctrl+wheel: zoom
  • Tap-record on + playing: WASD record notes in that lane at the playhead
  • Ctrl+Z undo

Saving

Save to game stores the map in this browser (the game loads it instead of the shipped file). To make it permanent, Download JSON and replace assets/beatmaps/feltwood_requiem.json, and Download .beatmap.js to replace the file:// copy next to it.
